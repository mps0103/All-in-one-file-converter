# Privacy Policy — All-in-One File Converter

**Last updated:** 15 October 2026
**App:** All-in-One File Converter (`com.mps.allinone`)
**Developer:** MPS
**Contact:** info@dealtrix.com

## The short version

Your files never leave your phone. Every conversion, compression, edit and preview runs on the device. We do not have a server, we do not have an account system, and we cannot see anything you convert, view or play.

## Files you convert, view and play

When you pick a file, Android gives the app temporary read access to that one file. The app reads it, converts it on the device, and writes the result into your Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded, copied to us, or shared with anyone.

Files you open in the built-in viewer (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, images and archive contents) are shown on the device only. Audio you preview while trimming or making a ringtone is played on the device only.

The app keeps a short list inside the app of files it made, so you can reopen them. That list holds only the file name, the format, the time and the Android reference to the file. It stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere. You can clear it from **Files → Clear list**.

So that a file you viewed can still be opened later, Android is asked to keep read access to that one file after the app closes. This is per file, granted by you when you pick it, and released when you clear the list or uninstall the app. It gives access to nothing else on the device.

## Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Internet, network state | Only to load ads and to check your purchase with Google Play. Conversion works with the phone in airplane mode. |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) | Used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads. Not used if you buy premium. |
| Billing | To sell and restore the premium subscription or the one-time unlock through Google Play. |
| Write external storage (Android 9 and older only) | To save the converted file into Downloads. Android 10 and newer do not need it. |
| Vibrate | A very short tap feedback when you switch options. |
| Camera (only if your phone asks for it) | Used only when you tap **Camera** in Image to PDF to scan pages. |

The app does **not** request contacts, location, microphone, call logs, SMS, or all-files access.

## Camera scanning

When you tap **Camera** in Image to PDF, the document scanner from Google Play services opens. It finds the page edges, crops and straightens the photo on your phone, and hands the image back to the app to build the PDF. The photos are not uploaded by the app, and the camera is never used in the background or without you tapping Camera.

Ringtones you make are saved into the phone's Ringtones folder, where you can choose them in your phone's sound settings. Text read from pictures (Image to Text) is recognised by an on-device model; the picture is never uploaded.

Archives are unpacked into a folder inside Downloads. The unpacked files stay on your phone and are never read, indexed or uploaded by the app.

## Ads

The app shows only one kind of ad: an optional **rewarded video** from **Google AdMob**. There are no banner ads and no full-screen ads that appear on their own. AdMob may collect and process the advertising identifier, coarse device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent screen from Google's User Messaging Platform appears before any personalised ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time from **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.

A rewarded ad is offered only when your free conversions are used up, and you choose whether to watch it in exchange for one extra conversion. Watching one is always optional: nothing is unlocked by watching and nothing is taken away by declining. The count of conversions you have earned is stored on your phone only.

Buying premium removes all ads from the app, rewarded ads included.

## Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. We never see or store your card details, address, or any other payment information. The app stores only a yes or no flag on the device saying whether premium is active.

Subscriptions renew until cancelled. You can cancel or manage a subscription in the Play Store under **Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

## Data we collect

None that identifies you. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no account, no login, and no server owned by us. The only data leaving your device is the ad and billing traffic described above, and that goes to Google, not to us.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ad requests are configured with a content rating of **G** and are not tagged as child-directed. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will address it.

## Your choices

- Clear the files list inside the app at any time.
- Change or withdraw ad consent from **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.
- Uninstall the app to remove everything it stores on the device. Files already saved to Downloads stay where they are, because they are yours.
